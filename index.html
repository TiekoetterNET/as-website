We maintain an open peering policy. This network is available for public peering at the IXPs listed above. Peering requests are welcome.

We differentiate between physical (P), virtual (V) and tunneled (T) IXPs. We prefer to peer over physical IXPs where possible.

Please email peering@tiekoetter.net if you would like to establish a peering session over a shared IXP.

Peering is provided on a best-effort basis without any SLA. We reserve the right to modify, suspend, or terminate peering sessions at any time, without prior notice or justification.
